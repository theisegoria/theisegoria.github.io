1. Wavefunction density

Change the wave number and inspect nodes of a particle in a box.

Worked example. The density integrates to one after normalization.

Watch out. The plotted state is stationary; measurement collapse is not shown.

Where is the particle least likely?

At nodes where the probability density is zero.

2. Uncertainty principle

Compare narrow and broad Gaussian packets in position and momentum.

Worked example. A narrower packet has a broader momentum distribution.

Watch out. The plotted widths are standard deviations of an ideal Gaussian.

What trade-off is visible?

Localizing x increases spread in p.

Reference: MIT OpenCourseWare · Quantum Physics II

3. Bloch sphere

Rotate a qubit state in a small 3D scene; the vector tip records the amplitudes and phase.

Worked example. The poles are |0⟩ and |1⟩; the equator contains equal superpositions.

Watch out. The sphere is a state-space representation, not a particle orbit in real space.

What does azimuth encode?

The relative phase between the basis amplitudes.
