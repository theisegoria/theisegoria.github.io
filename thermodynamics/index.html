Predict, manipulate, then check your reasoning against the example and question. Graphs illustrate the mathematics; they do not replace a proof.

1. PV diagrams

Drag a piston path and see work as the signed area under the curve.

Worked example. Expansion at positive pressure does work on the surroundings.

Watch out. The path matters; work is not a state function.

Which quantity is path-dependent here?

Work and heat, unlike internal energy.

Reference: MIT OpenCourseWare · Thermodynamics

2. Entropy and reversibility

Compare a reversible ramp with an irreversible jump between two temperatures.

Worked example. Reversible exchange can approach zero entropy production.

Watch out. A reversible process is an ideal limit, not a fast practical operation.

What direction is irreversible entropy production?

It is nonnegative for the universe.

Reference: MIT OpenCourseWare · Thermodynamics

3. Equation of state

Change amount, temperature, and volume for an ideal gas and read pressure.

Worked example. Doubling temperature at fixed n and V doubles pressure.

Watch out. Real gases deviate near phase transitions and high density.

Which variables are intensive?

Temperature and pressure; n and V scale with system size.
