Lebesgue integration, from measure to calculation.

I teach the Lebesgue integral by contrast with the Riemann integral, and I lead with diagrams: measurable sets, simple functions, signed integrals, convergence theorems, Tonelli and Fubini, with seven fully worked examples and a final practice set.
