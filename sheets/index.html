Mathematics · Print and keep

Reference sheets

Dense single-subject sheets meant to sit open beside you while you work: the identities, the transformations that generate them, and the traps, with nothing in between.

Each one is typeset in LaTeX and sized for paper rather than a screen. They are deliberately not tutorials — the explanatory material lives in the guides and the longer book editions. Every sheet is published in English and Japanese.

How they are made

A sheet earns its place by what it leaves out.

Everything here is compiled from LaTeX sources: Palatino for the mathematics, Noto Serif CJK for the Japanese editions. The sheets are laid out in two columns on A4 and print cleanly in black and white, which is the only test that really matters for a page you intend to keep beside you.
