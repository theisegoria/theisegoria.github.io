Reference sheets

I write these as dense single-subject sheets, meant to sit open beside you while you work: the identities, the transformations that generate them, and the traps, with nothing in between.

I typeset each one in LaTeX and size it for paper rather than a screen. I left the teaching out of them deliberately: the explanatory material lives in the guides and the longer book editions. I publish every sheet in English and Japanese.

How they are made

A sheet earns its place by what it leaves out.

I compile everything here from LaTeX sources: Palatino for the mathematics, Noto Serif CJK for the Japanese editions. I lay the sheets out in two columns on A4 and check that they print cleanly in black and white, which is the only test I trust for a page you intend to keep beside you.
