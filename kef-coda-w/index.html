THE ACOUSTIC IDEA

Bring the origins
of sound together.

In a conventional two-way layout, the tweeter and bass/midrange driver occupy different positions. Around the frequencies they share, moving above or below the speaker changes the distance from each driver to your ears.

Uni-Q aims to bring their acoustic origins together. The surrounding cone also forms part of the tweeter’s waveguide. The intent is a smoother transition as you move away from the central listening position. KEF’s Uni-Q explanation ↗

Coincidence does not make a speaker omnidirectional, remove room reflections, or guarantee identical sound in every seat. Real performance still depends on driver directivity, crossover design, cabinet diffraction and the room.
