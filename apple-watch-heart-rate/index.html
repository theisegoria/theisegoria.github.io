Optical pulse ≠ ECG

PPG tracks blood-volume changes at the wrist. The ECG feature on supported watches measures electrical activity using the back electrode and a finger on the Digital Crown. This optical experiment does not simulate ECG or blood oxygen.

What this model leaves out

Exterior proportions follow the 42 mm Series 10 (36 × 42 × 9.7 mm). The rear layout follows Apple’s representative third-generation sensor diagram. Tissue, vessel size, ray paths, signal amplitudes and noise are illustrative; the cutaway gap is an inspection aid. The noise examples deliberately withhold an estimate; this is not a validated quality threshold. No live health data is collected, and Apple’s proprietary estimator is not reproduced.
