Support

Common questions
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Problems are generated on device and the mathematics typesetting engine is bundled inside the app. It works fully offline, including on a plane.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-in, no server, and no account of any kind.
What data is collected?
None. Your progress is stored only on your device. See the privacy policy.
Are the problems repeated from a question bank?
No. Each family draws its own parameters and computes the answer in code, so the pool is effectively unbounded and every answer is correct by construction.
Is there a subscription?
No. It is a one-off purchase with no in-app purchases, no subscription, and no advertising.
How do I report a problem or a wrong answer?
Open an issue on the release repository, including the problem title and what you expected.
