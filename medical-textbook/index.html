Integrated Medical Foundations

I am writing a complete 95-chapter concise edition in English and Japanese, plus a bilingual pre-clinical edition that I keep expanding. I treat the Markdown files as the canonical text and the source I narrate from; the reflowable EPUB3 files are suitable for Kindle ingestion and Kobo sideloading.

Anki study cards: early draft

Expanded edition preview

In the latest checkpoint, v0.23, I added a respiratory-examination walkthrough to Chapter 16 in both languages, covering reproducible localisation, side-to-side comparison, and the limits of what an examination can tell you. In this pass I am working on clinically applied physiology, disease mechanisms, and anatomy explained through verbal spatial walkthroughs. Earlier additions are all still there, and the chapter and narration-section counts below are unchanged. These are drafts I am writing as a student, not clinical guidance.

Checkpoint v0.11 contains 104 chapters and 244 corresponding narration sections in each language, including the introductory modules in Chapters 1–68. In that update I deepened Chapters 1 and 2 with fluid compartments, concentration versus total amount, refractory periods, and action-potential propagation. Chapters 96 through 104 add pathology, pharmacology, behavioural and social science, communication, biostatistics, experimental methods, social determinants, health systems, global burden, planetary health, and exercise and extreme-environment physiology. I aim each module at approximately ten minutes, and I am still checking durations and reviewing the translation across the whole book.
