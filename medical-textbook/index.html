Integrated Medical Foundations

I am building an English–Japanese study resource from MCAT foundations through pre-clinical medicine. The expanded draft now has 105 chapters and 246 corresponding TTS modules per language, alongside an earlier 95-chapter concise edition. Markdown is the canonical text. Complete expanded EPUBs preserve that text and pass EPUBCheck; Kobo rendering and Kindle conversion still need testing.
