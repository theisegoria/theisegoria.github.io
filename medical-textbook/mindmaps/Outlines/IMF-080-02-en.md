# Neck, pharynx, larynx, thyroid, and deep spaces

## Triangles and fascia
### Sternocleidomastoid divides anterior and posterior triangles
#### Anterior: submental, submandibular, carotid, muscular
#### Posterior: divided by inferior omohyoid
### Investing layer encloses sternocleidomastoid, trapezius
### Pretracheal fascia: thyroid, trachea, oesophagus
### Prevertebral fascia extends as axillary sheath
### Carotid sheath: carotid, internal jugular, vagus
#### Sympathetic trunk behind, in prevertebral fascia
### Retropharyngeal and danger spaces
#### Infection descends to mediastinum

## Muscles and cervical roots
### Sternocleidomastoid turns face contralaterally
### Accessory nerve supplies sternocleidomastoid, trapezius
### Suprahyoids elevate hyoid or depress mandible
### Infrahyoids stabilise and depress hyoid
#### Ansa cervicalis C1 to C3
### Mylohyoid, anterior digastric: mandibular nerve
### Posterior digastric, stylohyoid: facial nerve
### Plexus and subclavian artery between scalenes
#### Subclavian vein anterior to anterior scalene
#### Phrenic nerve descends on anterior scalene

## Pharynx and swallowing
### Skull base to cricoid near C6
### Constrictors propel, longitudinal muscles elevate
### Vagus supplies most muscles
#### Stylopharyngeus glossopharyngeal
### Sensation: maxillary, glossopharyngeal, vagus by region
### Swallowing sequence
#### Palate closes, hyoid and larynx elevate
#### Vocal folds close, upper sphincter relaxes
#### Protection needs timing and sensation
### Piriform recess traps foreign bodies
#### Removal can injure internal laryngeal nerve

## Laryngeal skeleton
### C3 to C6, suspended from hyoid
### Cricoid complete ring, narrow in children
### Cricothyroid joint tilt changes fold tension
### Cricoarytenoid joint positions vocal process
### Internal laryngeal nerve pierces thyrohyoid membrane
### Cricothyroid membrane: emergency airway access

## Laryngeal muscles and nerves
### Posterior cricoarytenoid: only major abductor
### Lateral cricoarytenoid and arytenoids adduct
### Cricothyroid lengthens and tenses folds
### Recurrent nerve supplies all but cricothyroid
#### Sensation below folds
### Internal superior laryngeal: sensation above folds
### Left recurrent loops under aortic arch
#### Longer course exposed to mediastinal disease
### Unilateral palsy: dysphonia, paramedian fold
### Bilateral injury threatens airway
### External branch injury impairs pitch

## Thyroid, parathyroids, and trachea
### Isthmus anterior to second to fourth rings
### Pretracheal sheath links gland to larynx
#### Thyroid moves with swallowing
### Superior thyroid artery near external laryngeal nerve
### Inferior thyroid artery varies with recurrent nerve
### Inferior parathyroids vary from thymic descent
#### Ectopic glands from jaw to mediastinum
### Tracheostomy: cricoid, isthmus, vessels, pleura

## Oesophagus and major vessels
### Oesophagus begins at cricopharyngeus near C6
### Killian region: weak site for diverticulum
### Carotid bifurcation near upper thyroid cartilage
#### Carotid sinus senses pressure
#### Carotid body senses blood gases
### Internal carotid has no neck branches
### Internal jugular joins subclavian behind joint
### Open neck veins can entrain air

## Deep neck infection and airway
### Mylohyoid divides submandibular space
#### Bilateral cellulitis elevates tongue
### Submandibular duct crossed by lingual nerve
### Stones favour long ascending submandibular duct
### Parotid: nerve, vein, artery from superficial
#### Secretomotor via otic ganglion
### Peritonsillar infection causes trismus
### Parapharyngeal space near carotid sheath
### Danger signs: stridor, drooling, secretions
#### Plan airway before manipulation

## Clinical synthesis
### Longitudinal conduits crossed by mobile structures
### Know the expanding space and route to airway
