# Neural coding, synapses, sensory systems, and pain

## Electrical coding
### Ion gradients and permeability set resting potential
### Threshold opens voltage-gated sodium channels
#### Inactivation and potassium efflux repolarise
### Refractory periods limit frequency and direction
### Myelin enables saltatory conduction
#### Larger diameter also speeds conduction
### All-or-none amplitude, so intensity by rate and recruitment
### Rapid adaptation signals change, slow signals duration

## Synaptic transmission and integration
### Depolarisation opens presynaptic calcium channels
#### Calcium triggers vesicle fusion and release
### Ionotropic fast, metabotropic slow and amplified
### Inhibition via chloride or potassium conductance
### Axon initial segment integrates thousands of inputs
### Presynaptic inhibition alters release
### Lateral and reciprocal inhibition sharpen contrast
### Glutamate excites, GABA inhibits in brain
#### Glycine in spinal cord and brainstem
### Effect depends on receptor and circuit, not name

## Plasticity and glia
### Long-term potentiation and depression
#### Receptor trafficking, kinases, genes, structure
### Supports learning, also addiction, pain, epilepsy
### Astrocytes regulate ions, clearance, blood flow
### Oligodendrocytes central, Schwann cells peripheral
### Microglia survey, inflame, prune

## Sensory organisation
### Small fields and lateral inhibition aid discrimination
### Overlapping population codes refine localisation
### Cortical maps favour important regions and reorganise
### Dorsal columns: touch, vibration, proprioception
#### Ipsilateral ascent, cross in medulla
### Anterolateral system: pain, temperature, itch
#### Cross near spinal entry
### Spindles sense length, tendon organs sense tension
#### Sensory ataxia worsens without vision

## Vision
### Cornea refracts most, lens accommodates
### Photoreceptors hyperpolarise in light
#### Rods for dim light, cones for acuity and colour
### Nasal fibres cross at chiasm
#### Each optic tract carries the opposite field
### Lesion site predicts field defect pattern
### Pupillary pathway diverges before cortex
#### Light response kept in cortical blindness
### Accommodation: ciliary contraction, lens rounding, convergence

## Hearing and balance
### Place coding: high at base, low at apex
### Inner hair cells output, outer hair cells amplify
### Bilateral central relay
#### Unilateral central lesion rarely deafens one ear
### Conductive versus sensorineural loss
### Canals sense angular acceleration
### Utricle and saccule sense linear acceleration and tilt
### Sensory mismatch causes vertigo and nystagmus

## Taste, smell, and visceral sensation
### Five tastes via ion channels or G proteins
### Smell projects to bulb and limbic-linked cortex
#### Links odour to memory and emotion
### Visceral afferents: stretch, chemistry, ischaemia
### Sparse, convergent input makes pain diffuse
#### Referred pain follows shared spinal segments

## Nociception and pain
### Free nerve endings sense danger
### Injury mediators lower thresholds
### A-delta sharp and localised
### C fibres burning, aching, affective
### Pain integrates salience, fear, expectation, control
#### Real without proportionate visible damage
### Descending control inhibits or facilitates
#### Placebo and nocebo use genuine physiology

## Pain types and sensitisation
### Peripheral sensitisation lowers thresholds
### Central sensitisation amplifies and enlarges fields
#### Innocuous input becomes painful
### Neuropathic: lesion of somatosensory system
### Nociplastic: altered processing without sufficient damage
### Treat mechanism and function, not intensity alone

## Analgesic principles
### Paracetamol: limited anti-inflammatory action
#### Overdose causes hepatic injury
### Non-steroidal drugs reduce prostaglandin synthesis
#### Bleeding, kidney injury, fluid, cardiovascular harm
### Local anaesthetics block sodium channels
### Opioids: sedation, respiratory depression, dependence
### Neuropathic: antidepressants, channel ligands, topicals
### Non-drug care: explanation, activity, sleep, rehab
### Multimodal care improves function, limits harm
