# Perineum, genitalia, and reproductive organs

## Perineal triangles and pouches
### Diamond from symphysis to coccyx
### Intertuberous line divides two triangles
### Perineal membrane spans pubic arch
### Superficial pouch: genital roots, muscles
#### Greater vestibular glands when present
### Deep pouch: external urethral sphincter
#### Bulbourethral glands in male anatomy
### Ischioanal fossae: fat permits anal expansion
#### Pudendal canal in obturator fascia
#### Infection crosses behind anal canal

## Perineal fascia and spread
### Membranous fascia continues with Scarpa, dartos
#### Attachment below inguinal ligament spares thigh
### Spongy urethral rupture
#### Urine to scrotum, penis, abdominal wall
#### Not freely into thighs or anal triangle
### Membranous or bladder injury: deep planes
### Necrotising infection spreads along planes

## Erectile tissue and control
### Vascular spaces within tunica
### Cavernosa form crura on ischiopubic rami
### Spongiosum surrounds spongy urethra
### Clitoral corpora, vestibular bulbs
### Ischiocavernosus reduces venous outflow
### Bulbospongiosus: ejaculation, vaginal constriction
### Parasympathetic cavernous nerves
#### Nitric oxide dilates arteries, erection
### Sympathetic: emission, detumescence
### Dorsal nerve somatic, autonomic nerves deeper

## Testes, cord, and ducts
### Descent through inguinal canal
#### Coverings from abdominal wall layers
#### Tunica vaginalis from processus vaginalis
### Tubules to rete, epididymis, ductus
### Cord contents
#### Ilioinguinal nerve outside coverings
### Arteries from aorta
#### Left vein to left renal vein
#### Lymph to para-aortic nodes
### Ductus crosses ureter, joins seminal duct

## Prostate and accessory glands
### Surrounds proximal urethra below bladder
### Peripheral, central, transitional, anterior zones
#### Carcinoma mostly peripheral
#### Benign enlargement transitional
### Ejaculatory ducts open near colliculus
### Venous plexus to vertebral plexus
### Seminal glands: fructose, no sperm storage

## Ovaries, tubes, uterus, vagina
### Ovary in ovarian fossa, position varies
#### Suspensory ligament carries ovarian vessels
#### Ovarian vessels and lymph to aorta
### Tube: fimbriae, ampulla, isthmus
#### Fertilisation commonly in ampulla
#### Impaired transport: ectopic pregnancy
### Uterus commonly anteverted, anteflexed
### Broad ligament not main support
#### Cardinal, uterosacral, floor support
### Uterine artery crosses above ureter
### Posterior fornix deepest
### Lower vagina somatic, upper autonomic

## External genitalia and glands
### Vulva: labia, clitoris, vestibule, glands
#### Size and pigmentation vary widely
### Penis: paired cavernosa, ventral spongiosum
#### Deep dorsal vein to prostatic plexus
### Cremaster via genitofemoral motor fibres
### Temperature: skin, dartos, pampiniform exchange

## Childbirth, surgery, and pain
### Birth stretches floor and perineal body
#### Nerves, levator, sphincter may tear
#### Repair identifies sphincter, rectal injury
### Hysterectomy risks ureter
### Prostate, rectal surgery risk cavernous nerves
### Node dissection risks obturator nerve
### Perineal pain has many sources
#### Localise by organ, not gender
