# Reproductive, skin, sensory, and lymphoid histology

## Spatial organisation
### Reproductive tissue varies with age and cycle
### Sensory epithelia transduce stimuli
### Lymphoid compartments organise immune cells

## Testis
### Tunica albuginea, lobules, seminiferous tubules
### Germ cells mature from base to lumen
#### Mitosis, meiosis, differentiation
### Sertoli cells span basement membrane to lumen
#### Tight junctions form blood–testis barrier
#### Inhibin and androgen-binding protein
### Leydig cells secrete testosterone

## Male ducts and glands
### Efferent ductules: scalloped lumen
### Epididymis: stereocilia, sperm maturation
### Ductus deferens: thick three-layered muscle
### Seminal vesicle stores no sperm
### Prostate: corpora amylacea increase with age
### Erectile tissue: endothelial vascular spaces

## Ovary
### Cortex follicles, vascular medulla
### Primordial: one layer of flattened cells
### Primary: cuboidal granulosa, zona pellucida
### Antral: fluid cavity, theca layers
### Corpus luteum makes progesterone
#### Without support becomes corpus albicans
### Atresia far exceeds ovulation

## Tube, uterus, cervix, vagina, breast
### Tube: ciliated and peg cells, ampulla folds
#### Obstruction: infertility, ectopic implantation
### Basalis regenerates functionalis
### Proliferative: straight mitotic glands
### Secretory: vacuoles, tortuous glands
### Endocervix columnar, ectocervix squamous
#### Junction vulnerable to papillomavirus
### Vagina: glycogen-rich, no glands
### Breast proliferates in pregnancy
#### Myoepithelial cells aid milk ejection

## Skin
### Strata from basale to corneum
#### Corneocytes form the barrier
### Melanin shields nuclei from ultraviolet
#### Colour reflects melanosomes, not cell number
### Langerhans cells present antigen
### Papillary loose, reticular dense dermis
### Thick skin lacks hair and sebaceous glands
### Sebaceous glands are holocrine
### Eccrine ducts reabsorb salt

## Eye and ear
### Cornea transparent from orderly collagen
#### Endothelium pumps fluid from stroma
##### Cell loss causes oedema, opacity
### Photoreceptors to bipolar to ganglion cells
### Fovea maximises cone resolution
### Optic disc lacks photoreceptors
### Retinal vessels inner, choroid outer
### Inner hair cells transduce sound
### Outer hair cells amplify vibration
### Maculae linear, cristae angular acceleration

## Taste and smell
### Taste receptor cells extend microvilli to pore
### Olfactory axons cross cribriform plate
### Olfactory receptor cells are replaced

## Lymphoid organs
### Lymph node filters lymph
#### Cortex: B-cell follicles, germinal centres
#### Paracortex: T cells, high endothelial venules
#### Medullary cords hold plasma cells
### Spleen filters blood
#### White pulp around central arterioles
#### Red pulp retains rigid erythrocytes
### Thymus supports T-cell development
#### Epithelial reticular cells, Hassall corpuscles
#### No follicles or afferent lymphatics
### Tonsils and Peyer patches sample antigen
#### Microfold cells transport luminal material
