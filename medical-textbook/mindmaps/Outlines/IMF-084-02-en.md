# Gut, liver, pancreas, kidney, endocrine histology

## Gut wall plan
### Mucosa, submucosa, muscularis externa, serosa
#### Mucosa: epithelium, lamina propria, muscularis
#### Submucosal plexus in submucosa
#### Myenteric plexus between muscle layers
### Serosa is mesothelium, adventitia binds

## Oesophagus and stomach
### Oesophagus: non-keratinised stratified squamous
#### Skeletal to smooth muscle downward
#### Reflux causes columnar metaplasia
### Gastric pits lined by surface mucous cells
### Parietal cells: acid and intrinsic factor
### Chief cells: basophilic, pepsinogen
### Fundus and body: short pits, long glands
### Pylorus: deep pits, G and D cells
### Inner oblique layer aids mixing

## Small intestine
### Plicae, villi, microvilli amplify surface
### Villus: lacteal, capillaries, smooth muscle
### Crypts hold stem and Paneth cells
#### Paneth granules are antimicrobial
### Enteroendocrine cells release hormones basally
### Duodenum: Brunner glands
### Jejunum: prominent folds, long villi
### Ileum: Peyer patches into submucosa

## Large intestine and anal canal
### No villi, straight goblet-rich crypts
### Taeniae coli except appendix and rectum
### Appendix: extensive lymphoid tissue
### Anal canal: columnar to keratinised skin

## Liver and gallbladder
### Blood portal to central, bile opposite
### Portal tract: vein, artery, bile duct
### Sinusoids mix portal and arterial blood
#### Fenestrated, no continuous basement membrane
#### Space of Disse for exchange
### Kupffer cells: sinusoidal macrophages
### Stellate cells store vitamin A
#### Chronic injury: matrix-producing cells
### Canaliculi sealed by tight junctions
### Acinus zones explain hypoxic vulnerability
### Gallbladder concentrates bile
#### No muscularis mucosae or submucosa

## Pancreas
### Serous acini with zymogen granules
### Centroacinar cells begin intercalated ducts
### No striated ducts, unlike salivary glands
### Enzyme activation damages acini and fat
### Islet cell types need immunolabelling
#### Beta insulin, alpha glucagon

## Kidney
### Cortex corpuscles, medulla loops and ducts
### Podocytes wrap capillaries with foot processes
### Barrier: endothelium, basement membrane, slits
#### Injury target shapes the pattern
### Mesangial cells support and clear
### Proximal tubule: brush border, eosinophilic
### Distal tubule: pale, clear lumen
### Collecting ducts: distinct cell boundaries
### Juxtaglomerular apparatus
#### Macula densa senses sodium chloride
#### Granular cells release renin

## Endocrine glands
### Anterior pituitary: cords around sinusoids
### Posterior pituitary: axons, Herring bodies
### Thyroid follicles filled with colloid
#### Iodination apical, release basal
#### Cell height reflects activity
### C cells secrete calcitonin
### Parathyroid chief cells secrete hormone
### Adrenal cortex: three zones
#### Glomerulosa: mineralocorticoids
#### Fasciculata: glucocorticoids
#### Reticularis: androgens
### Medulla: chromaffin cells

## Tracing flow
### Food, portal blood, bile, filtrate, hormones
### Direction links form to physiology
