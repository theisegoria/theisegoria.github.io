# Connective tissue, blood, cartilage, bone, and fat

## Connective tissue plan
### Support, bind, nourish, defend, store, transmit force
### Abundant extracellular matrix between cells
### Properties from fibres, ground substance, minerals, cells
### Develops from embryonic mesenchyme
### Fibroblasts produce matrix
#### Active cells: more basophilic cytoplasm
#### Become myofibroblasts after injury

## Fibres and ground substance
### Collagen resists tension
#### Type one: thick fibres in dermis, tendon, bone, scar
#### Type two: cartilage and vitreous body
#### Type three: reticular fibres of cellular organs
#### Type four: basement-membrane sheets
### Elastic fibres: elastin core, fibrillin microfibrils
#### Arterial lamellae store energy in systole
#### Fibrillin defects: skeleton, eye, aorta
#### Elastin degradation: emphysema, arterial change
### Ground substance is a hydrated gel
#### Negative glycosaminoglycans attract cations and water
#### Hyaluronan occupies space, aids migration
#### Fibronectin links cells, laminins organise membrane
#### Poorly preserved, looks like empty space

## Connective tissue types
### Loose: diffusion and immune surveillance
#### Readily develops oedema
### Dense irregular resists multidirectional stress
### Dense regular aligns collagen with force
### Tendon: parallel bundles, tenocytes, few vessels
#### Tensile strength but slow healing
#### Repetitive load: disorganisation, failed repair
### Ligaments join bones, may add elastic fibres

## Resident cells and adipose tissue
### Macrophages phagocytose, present antigen, remodel
### Mast cells release histamine and proteases
### Plasma cells: clock-face nucleus, pale Golgi
### White fat: one large droplet, signet ring
#### Stores triglyceride, secretes adipokines
#### Visceral and subcutaneous depots differ
### Brown fat: many droplets, abundant mitochondria
#### Uncoupling protein one leaks protons
#### Heat instead of adenosine triphosphate

## Blood
### Plasma is the fluid matrix
### Erythrocytes: anucleate biconcave discs
#### Pale centre about one third
#### Size, colour, shape, inclusions as clues
### Neutrophils dominate acute bacterial inflammation
### Eosinophils: parasite defence, allergy
### Monocytes become macrophage-like cells in tissue
### Platelets: fragments of megakaryocytes
#### Adhere, activate, recruit, support coagulation
#### In-vitro clumping mimics a low count

## Cartilage
### Chondrocytes in lacunae, avascular matrix
#### Nutrients diffuse from perichondrium or synovial fluid
#### Poor repair in adult articular cartilage
### Hyaline: fine type two fibrils
#### Articular cartilage lacks perichondrium
### Elastic cartilage: pinna and epiglottis
### Fibrocartilage resists tension plus compression

## Bone structure and formation
### Mineralised support, calcium and phosphate store
### Osteoblasts secrete osteoid
### Osteocytes sense load through canaliculi
### Osteoclasts resorb in sealed acid compartments
### Osteons: lamellae around a central canal
#### Interstitial lamellae are old osteon remnants
### Trabecular bone aligns with load
### Woven bone remodelled into lamellar bone
### Intramembranous or endochondral ossification

## Growth, remodelling, and repair
### Growth plate zones
#### Columns divide, enlarge, die, are replaced
#### Plate closure ends longitudinal growth
### Remodelling couples resorption and formation
#### Ligand and decoy receptor steer osteoclasts
#### Hormones, loading, inflammation shift balance
### Repair: haemostasis through remodelling
#### Granulation tissue is not granulomatous
#### Type three collagen replaced, strength rises
### Healing follows vascularity, cellularity, turnover
