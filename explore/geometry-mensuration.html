Triangles, Pythagoras and sectors

A triangle has base 6 and height 4. What is its area?

A = ½ · 6 · 4 = 12.

Regular polygons

A regular hexagon is inscribed in a circle of radius 4. How long is each side?

a = 2R sin(π/6) = 4, equal to the radius.

Cone, cylinder and sphere

A cone has radius 3 and height 4. What is its slant height?

ℓ = √(3² + 4²) = 5.

Scaling lengths, areas and volumes

Every length of a solid is doubled. By what factor does the volume change?

2³ = 8 times.
