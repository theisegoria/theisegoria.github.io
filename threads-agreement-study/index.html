Threads: agreement, disagreement, praise and criticism

An anonymized observational study of reply stance, account patterns, praise and criticism, with context from four behavioral studies.

Read the report

English · 8-page color PDF日本語 · 8ページ・カラーPDF

What the study covers

The broad sample contains 233 replies. Among the 48 classified as pure agreement or disagreement, 36 agree and 12 disagree: a 3:1 ratio. A separate, deliberately selected sample examines 120 replies and 26 authored posts from six accounts.

Four published studies examine when disagreement and emotionally charged conflict attract engagement. Their evidence is kept separate from the Threads observations.

This convenience sample does not estimate platform-wide ratios or causal effects. Observed accounts use numbered labels; account names and profile links are omitted.
