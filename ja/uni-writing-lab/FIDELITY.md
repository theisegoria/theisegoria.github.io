## Corrected landmarks
- DIVE cap seam is 41.5% from the capped nose, compared with approximately 41.6–41.7% in two official profiles. Rounded hexagonal cap and barrel have curved corners, a larger forward body, and a slimmer rear barrel. Cap-to-rear-body diameter ratio is approximately 1.12.
- DIVE clip is a swept tapered spring blade with raised shoulder and upturned toe. Cap opening has a hollow dark liner. Endpieces carry narrow axial ribs and slim metal rings.
- DIVE grip uses three axial segments along each circumferential row, followed by a smooth adjustment band, wrapped MIN/MID/MAX markings, metal collar and index recess, stepped nose and fine guide pipe. Engine window faces 90 degrees around the body from the clip.
- JETSTREAM has a long gently tapered rubber forebody, truncated nose, small retractable metal point, grip seam approximately 48% from the point, matching plastic clip with a spring gap, narrow rounded pushbutton and wrapped lettering.
- Profile, reverse, clip and tip cameras provide inspection beyond the hero angle. Tip view removes the detached cap from the close-up.

## Accuracy limits
The photos establish visible proportions and feature placement, not manufacturing tolerances. Scene units use nominal centimetre envelopes (about 14 cm pen and 14.25 cm capped pencil) for construction only. Conflicting pen listing dimensions and absent verified primary dimensional drawings prevent dimensional certification. Clip cross sections, subtle seams, moulded artwork, colours, cap liner and window contents remain photo-derived approximations. Internals in the separate mechanism diagrams are explicitly illustrative. No claim of measured 1:1 CAD accuracy is made.
