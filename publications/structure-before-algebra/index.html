Inside the book

Every technique carries its own recognition cue.

Each numbered technique gives the structural trigger that should bring it to mind, the mechanism in as few sentences as it takes, one fully worked instance, and the specific way it is most often botched. Every chapter closes with a recognition matrix.

I

The method

Reading a problem, the order of attack, camouflage as the real source of difficulty, and the verification ladder that tells you your answer is an answer.

lim

Limits and series

Dominance, the indeterminate forms, expansion as the primary engine, Riemann sums in disguise, recursion and fixed points, convergence tests and series evaluation.

d

Derivatives

Simplifying before differentiating, logarithmic differentiation, implicit and parametric structure, n-th derivative patterns, applied setups, and the traps that collapse.

∫

Integrals

Structural rewriting, the substitution families, parts as a machine, symmetry and reflection, Feynman and Frullani, series interchange, the special-functions ladder.

Δ

Differential equations

The classification decision, integrating factors, exact equations, constant-coefficient theory, Cauchy-Euler, systems and phase planes, transforms, models, and existence.

∀

Logic and puzzles

Formal logic as a tool, proof method as search strategy, invariants and monovariants, pigeonhole, counting, games, probability, number theory, and information bounds.

Editorial note

Built from a verified technique inventory

The book was assembled from a working corpus of solution archetypes: the ninety-six core generator families and several hundred catalogued technique variants, deduplicated into the inventory that organises these chapters. Every closed form, limit, derivative, solution and count asserted in the text was checked numerically or by exhaustive enumeration before it was typeset, and a number of errors in the source catalogue were corrected in the process.

It is a companion to Volume I, The Complete Daily Integral Field Guide, which catalogues problem archetypes; this volume teaches the techniques that dissolve them. It is an independent educational publication. Readers should verify delicate identities and high-stakes solutions against primary mathematical sources.
