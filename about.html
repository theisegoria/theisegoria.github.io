Clarity is a form of design

Four commitments

01

Begin with structure

I think understanding starts before the formalism, so I open every piece by asking what kind of system I am looking at, which features matter, and what ought to stay invariant.

02

Draw the mechanism

I do not draw a diagram as decoration. I draw it as a second statement of the idea, check it against the mathematics, and keep redrawing until it explains why the thing actually works.

03

Work it through completely

Intuition only convinces me once it has survived an exact case, so I show the derivations, the measurements and the code in enough detail for you to reproduce, test and reuse them.

04

Keep the door open

I keep everything within reach: pages that open in the browser, PDFs to download, source on GitHub. Every guide and report I publish goes out in both English and Japanese.

Why the name

Isegoria means an equal right to speak.

I chose it because I want this to be an equal invitation into technical conversation, whatever the subject. I am not trying to take the sophistication out, only the needless opacity: definitions, pictures, derivations and working code should hold each other up.
