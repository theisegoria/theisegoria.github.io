About

Hi, I’m Benjamin Haire.

This page is a collection of esoterica I find interesting.

Pure mathematics first: a BSc in Australia, then an SM in the United States. After that a Diploma of Languages in Japanese, which is why half of this site is bilingual. Currently a medical student in Australia.

What holds my attention: cellular automata, algebraic varieties, differential geometry. On the engineering side, game engine technology, particularly AI integration in modern engines, and real-time simulation of fluids and constrained bodies: XPBD and the wider family of position-based and implicit solvers, particle-grid hybrids, and what has to be given up to hit a frame budget.

Clarity is a form of design

Four commitments

01

Begin with structure

Understanding starts before the formalism. Each piece asks what kind of system is present, which features matter, and what should remain invariant.

02

Draw the mechanism

A diagram is not decoration. It is another statement of the idea, checked against the mathematics and used to explain why the thing actually works.

03

Work it through completely

Intuition becomes useful when it survives an exact case. Derivations, measurements, and code are shown with enough detail to reproduce, test, and reuse.

04

Keep the door open

Everything is available directly: pages that open in the browser, PDFs to download, source on GitHub. Every guide and report is published in both English and Japanese.

Why the name

Isegoria means an equal right to speak.

Here it names an equal invitation into technical conversation, whatever the subject. The aim is not to remove sophistication, but to remove needless opacity: definitions, pictures, derivations, and working code should reinforce one another.
