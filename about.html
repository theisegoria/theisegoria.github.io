About this site

Clarity is a form of design.

Isegoria publishes interactive explanations, technical research, and the software behind both: rigorous enough to trust, composed carefully enough to enjoy.

The approach

Four commitments

01

Begin with structure

Understanding starts before the formalism. Each piece asks what kind of system is present, which features matter, and what should remain invariant.

02

Draw the mechanism

A diagram is not decoration. It is another statement of the idea, checked against the mathematics and used to explain why the thing actually works.

03

Work it through completely

Intuition becomes useful when it survives an exact case. Derivations, measurements, and code are shown with enough detail to reproduce, test, and reuse.

04

Keep the door open

Everything is available directly: pages that open in the browser, PDFs to download, source on GitHub. No accounts, paywalls, trackers, or proprietary readers between you and the material.

Why the name

Isegoria means an equal right to speak.

Here it names an equal invitation into technical conversation, whatever the subject. The aim is not to remove sophistication, but to remove needless opacity: definitions, pictures, derivations, and working code should reinforce one another.
