About the library

Mathematical clarity
is a form of design.

Isegoria is a growing collection of free visual textbooks: rigorous enough to trust, composed carefully enough to enjoy.

01

Begin with structure

Good technique starts before the algebra. Each guide asks what kind of object is present, which features matter, and what should remain invariant.

02

Draw the mathematics

A diagram is not decoration. It is another statement of the idea, checked against the formula and used to explain why the calculation works.

03

Calculate completely

Intuition becomes useful when it survives an exact example. Derivations are shown with enough detail to reproduce, test, and reuse.

04

Keep the door open

Every publication is available as a direct PDF. There are no accounts, paywalls, trackers, or proprietary readers between you and the material.

Why the name?

Isegoria means an equal right to speak.

Here it names an equal invitation to mathematical conversation. The aim is not to remove sophistication, but to remove needless opacity: definitions, pictures, proofs, and computations should reinforce one another.
